# Key Manager Support and Data Requests

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Key Manager is operated by BHUB and administered by **roge4087** on Discord.

For access help, privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, send a **Discord direct message to roge4087**. If direct messages are unavailable, ask a moderator in the BHUB Discord server to connect you with the operator, or contact the creator through [BHUB on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/BHUB4087).

## Membership or access problems

Include your Discord user ID, the feature you used, an approximate time, and the error or result shown by the bot. A screenshot can help, but hide private information before sending it.

Check that Patreon is connected to the Discord account you are using. Provider updates can take time to reach the bot. Repeated commands do not bypass refresh timing or cooldowns.

## Privacy or deletion requests

You can send this message privately to the operator:

> Hello, I would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of my Key Manager data. My Discord user ID is [your Discord user ID]. My request is [describe the request]. Please let me know what account verification is needed and whether this affects my linked benefits.

Requests are handled manually. The operator may ask for reasonable evidence that the account is yours and will respond within the time required by applicable law. Do not send a password, authentication token or full payment-card details to verify ownership.

A deletion request can cover local account-link and referral records, applicable service logs and feedback retained by BHUB. The operator will explain how relevant Luarmor access records and provider-held copies are handled, including any lawful retention exceptions. Discord, Patreon and other providers may require separate requests for information under their own control.

The bot periodically refreshes Patreon information. If you want to stop future processing of your Patreon-linked Discord identifier, disconnect the integration through Patreon and tell the operator as part of your request. This does not automatically erase existing records. Removing information needed to verify your membership can affect access to the associated benefits.

Deleting bot records does not cancel a paid subscription. Use the checkout provider's cancellation controls for billing changes.

## Security reports

Report suspected data exposure, unauthorized access or vulnerabilities privately to the operator. Describe the issue and how to reproduce it without accessing someone else's account or collecting unnecessary personal data. Do not post credentials or private membership records in a public GitHub issue or Discord channel.
